Two ways to play

No team? No problem.

Half of our leagues are draft leagues. You register on your own, tell us what you play, and captains build balanced teams from the pool. The other half are bring-your-own-team, for crews that already run together.

Draft

Register solo

  1. Build your player profile: positions, QB willingness, experience
  2. Request up to 2 teammates you want to be drafted with
  3. Come to open play so captains can see you
  4. Get drafted onto a balanced team
Bring your team

Bring your crew

  1. A captain creates the team and gets a join code
  2. Teammates register with the code, no approval wait
  3. Short on players? Free agents can fill your roster
  4. Roster caps and coed rules are checked as people join

Cardinals Cup

Two days at the Cardinals Flag Football Campus at State Farm Stadium. Over 500 athletes played in 2025. Entry includes a Cardinals game ticket and tailgate pass, and the women's 5v5 bracket plays for a $500 cash prize.

More than a game night

An LGBTQ-friendly league where the season ends with an all-star vote, an awards party and a highlight reel with your name on it.

All-star voting

Players vote in the app for rookie, all-star and veteran teams near the end of every season. Selections are revealed at the awards gathering.

Your highlights, your call

Game photos and clips are tagged to you. You choose whether they are public, league-only or never shared. Share to Instagram in one tap.

Need a sub? Be a sub.

Opt in as a sub for other nights and locations. Captains short a player can find you without a Facebook post.
